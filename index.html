    About the response columns. Calendar data records each person's reply to the invitation: Accepted, Declined, Maybe or No response. It does not prove anyone actually attended. The release copy says so in its header.

    How to redact

    Pick an exemption in Redact as, then:
    • click ⊘ beside a field or person to redact all of it
    • highlight words in a title or description, then click Redact selection
    • click Withhold entry to withhold the whole entry
    • click any yellow highlight to remove that redaction
    Suggested redactions are only suggestions. Nothing is redacted until you apply it.
